Tic-Trap-Toe

You already know how to play.
Winning is the hard part.

Six boards on a rotating 3D cube. Claim 3 faces to win — if the rotation lets you. Keep winning and it stops going easy on you.

How to Play

  • Drag to spin the cube
  • Click any empty cell on a visible face to place your mark
  • Get 3 in a row on a face to claim it
  • Claim 3 faces to win the match

The Twist

The cube never stops rotating. Faces drift in and out of reach — the board you're about to win might spin away before your next move.

vs Computer

The AI starts casual and becomes unbeatable as you rack up face wins across rounds.
